Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations

Announcement

The Protecting Children – Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations Non-Government Schools eLearn training will move from this learning management system on 31 August 2026.

Non-government school staff should access the training through their sector Learning Management System (LMS) using the link below:

For Catholic school staff go to: https://sso.cecv.catholic.edu.au/home/login?des=Absorb

For Independent school staff go to: https://islearn.is.vic.edu.au/

If you have already started the training here, you have up until midday 31 August to complete it via this LMS. Or, you can complete the training through your sector LMS from Monday 24 August.

Refreshed training will be available from late Term 3. Staff can complete the refreshed training this year if they choose. All staff will need to complete the updated training in 2027.

If you are working in an Early Childhood setting, this change will not impact you.

Early Childhood staff can continue to complete their sector specific Protecting Children - Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations Early Childhood training here.

For more information, visit the department’s Online learning for schools webpage.

We updated the Information Sharing and MARAM Online Learning System (LMS) in December 2025. You need to create a new account if you have not logged in since then.

Any account created prior to October 2025 will no longer be valid, and using previous login details to request a new password will not work.

You will find information about how to create a new account at Protecting children: Mandatory reporting and other obligations.

For information about early childhood education and care child protection and child safety training requirements, go to Child protection and child safety training in early childhood | vic.gov.au.

Early Childhood

Relevant roles include Long Day Care, Kindergarten, Outside of School Hours Care and other Early Childhood Educators

Non-Government Schools

Relevant roles include Independent School staff, Catholic School staff, and all Casual Relief staff (CRTs)
