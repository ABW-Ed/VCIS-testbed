Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations

Announcement

The Protecting Children – Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations Non-Government Schools eLearn training has moved.

Government and non-government school staff, including Casual Relief Teachers (CRTs) must now access the training through their sector learning management systems using the relevant link below:

  • Catholic school staff and CRTs employed in Catholic schools
    • Catholic school staff and CRTs can access the training on the VCEA Courses platform
  • Independent school staff and CRTs employed in independent schools
    • Independent school staff and CRTs can access the training on the ISLearn platform
  • Government school staff and CRTs employed in Government schools
    • Government school staff and CRTs with an education.vic.gov.au email address can access the training via eduPay/MyLearnED. Instructions on how to access the training are available at Online learning for schools.
  • CRTs without access to the Catholic, independent and Government schools learning management systems
    • CRTs without access to their sector specific learning management systems can use the “Schools” course (listed below) to complete training.

Please refer to the department’s Online learning for schools webpage for more information or contact student.engagement@education.vic.gov.au.

If you are working in an Early Childhood setting, this change will not impact you.

Early Childhood services can continue to complete their sector specific Protecting Children - Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations Early Childhood training here.

You will need to create a new account if you have not logged into this site since October 2025.

Any account created prior to October 2025 will no longer be valid, and using previous login details to request a new password will not work.

You will find information about how to create a new account at Protecting children: Mandatory reporting and other obligations.

For information about early childhood education and care child protection and child safety training requirements, go to Child protection and child safety training in early childhood | vic.gov.au.

Early Childhood

Relevant roles include Long Day Care, Kindergarten, Outside of School Hours Care and other Early Childhood Educators

Schools

Relevant roles include Casual Relief staff (CRTs) without access to the Catholic, independent and Government schools learning management systems.
