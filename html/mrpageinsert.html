PROTECT Training

Announcement

This learning management system hosts:

  • EC PROTECT eLearn training for Early childhood professionals
  • Schools PROTECT eLearn training for Casual Relief Teachers (CRTs) without access to a school sector learning management system
    School staff and CRTs with access to their sector learning management systems should complete the PROTECT Child Safety Training for their school sector, links are provided below.

Early childhood professionals

Early childhood professionals complete their Protecting Children – Mandatory Reporting and Other Obligations – Early Childhood (EC PROTECT) training here or click the relevant tile below.

For information about early childhood education and care child protection and child safety training requirements, go to Child protection and child safety training in early childhood | vic.gov.au.

School staff and CRTs

Access the PROTECT Child Safety Training through the learning management system for your school sector:

  • Government school staff and CRTs employed in Government schools
    • Staff and CRTs with an education.vic.gov.au email address can access the training via eduPay/MyLearnED.
    • Instructions on how to access the training are available at PROTECT Child Safety eLearn.
  • Catholic school staff and CRTs employed in Catholic schools
    • Access the training on the VCEA Courses platform
  • Independent school staff and CRTs employed in independent schools
    • Independent school staff and CRTs can access the training on the ISLearn platform
  • CRTs without access to the Catholic, independent and Government schools learning management systems
    • CRTs without access to their sector specific learning management systems can use the “Schools” training here or click the relevant tile below.
      For training requirements, to access instructions and other information, visit PROTECT Child Safety eLearn. For support, contact student.engagement@education.vic.gov.au..

Schools
